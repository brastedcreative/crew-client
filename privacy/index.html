Privacy Policy

Last updated October 22, 2026

goblin.io is a scheduling app run by Josh Brasted Photography LLC ("we", "us"). It is for photographers, editors, producers and clients working on events we cover. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

Who can use it

The app is invitation only. You can sign in only with an email address a producer has added to an event. It is not intended for children.

What we collect

  • Your email address and name, as your producer entered them, to sign you in and show your schedule.
  • Your schedule: the events, shifts, call times, locations and notes your producer assigns to you.
  • Things you send in the app: messages and issue reports to your producer, change requests from clients, and receipts for reimbursement, including any photos you attach.
  • A device token for notifications, if you allow notifications, so we can alert you when your schedule changes.
  • When you last signed in, so your producer can see that your invitation worked.

We do not collect your location, contacts or browsing activity. We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

How we use it

Only to run the schedule: showing you your events, sending you sign-in links, invitations and schedule alerts, and passing your messages, reports, requests and receipts to the producers running your event. We do not sell your information or use it for marketing.

Who else handles it

We use a few service providers to run the app. They process your information only on our behalf:

  • Supabase stores the app's data and handles sign-in.
  • Apple Push Notification service delivers notifications to your phone.
  • Resend sends invitation and sign-in emails.
  • Google (Sheets and Drive) and Airtable hold the production schedules your producer builds and syncs into the app.

Producers and editors on your event can see the information that relates to that event. Clients can see the coverage schedule and the status of their own requests, but not crew contact details or receipts.

How long we keep it

We keep event information while the event is being planned and delivered, and for business records afterward, such as receipts needed for accounting. You can ask us to delete your account and personal information at any time. We will do so unless we have to keep a record by law.

Your choices

  • Turn notifications off at any time in your iPhone's Settings.
  • Ask to see, correct or delete your information by emailing us.
  • Stop using the app and delete it from your phone. Ask us if you also want your account removed.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections and stored with access controls. Each person sees only the events they are on. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
